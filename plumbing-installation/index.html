Plumbing installation covers the work that adds or changes plumbing in a house: a new bathroom, a kitchen remodel, a laundry room move, an accessory dwelling unit, or a new fixture where there was none. For homeowners in Cupertino, CA we schedule these jobs with licensed plumbers, and the 24/7 line stays open for questions and for emergencies that arise during a remodel.

Good installation starts before the first pipe is cut. We review the plans, check the existing lines, confirm vent and drain slope, and agree on a scope, a schedule and a price.

Outdoor and utility lines

Hose bibs, laundry boxes and exterior supply.

Kitchen sink rough-in

A kitchen sink rough-in sets the supply stubs, the drain and the vent before cabinets and walls are finished. The drain height, the vent connection and the supply position all depend on the sink and cabinet. A wrong measurement at this stage becomes a costly fix after drywall.

We measure against the cabinet plans, set stub-outs at the right height, and install shutoff valves that you can reach. A garbage disposal and dishwasher connection are planned at the same time.

Bathroom additions and remodels

A bathroom addition needs a drain that slopes correctly, a vent that works, and supply lines that serve the new fixtures without starving the rest of the house. Shower valves, tub drains and toilet flanges are set at framing stage and tested before walls close.

We pressure test new lines, and we leave the system ready for inspection. See toilet service and faucet service for fixture-level work.

Permits and inspection in Cupertino

The Cupertino Building Division says plumbing permits are required for the installation or replacement of plumbing fixtures, including sewer, water and gas lines, water heaters, sinks and water closets. Stopping leaks and clearing stoppages generally do not need a permit. Work without a required permit can be stopped, and fees are doubled.

The final inspection cannot be scheduled until the contractor and subcontractors have a City of Cupertino business license. We prepare the permit, schedule rough and final inspections, and keep the work open until it passes.

Accessory dwelling units and additions

An accessory dwelling unit or a room addition needs a new water supply, a drain connection and often a water heater. The city and the sanitary district both matter, since the drain connects to a lateral and the capacity of that line is a question. Cupertino lists its sanitary providers on its utilities page, and we help you plan the connection.

We coordinate with your contractor or architect so plumbing is roughed in at the right time. See water heater installation for the heater side of the job.

Working with other trades

Plumbing is one phase among several. We coordinate with framers, electricians and tile setters so the work does not conflict. A shower pan, a tub, a vanity and a toilet all depend on the order of work, and a plumber who communicates keeps the schedule moving.

What affects installation pricing

Scope, number of fixtures, material, access, and permit fees set the price. A single fixture swap is a short job, and a room addition is a project. We give a clear price before work starts and note any items outside the scope.
