Privacy Policy

How we handle information when you use this website or call our line.

Calls to our line

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we ask for your address, a description of the problem and a callback number so a plumber can reach you. We use that information to dispatch service and to follow up on your request.

This website

This website has no forms and does not ask you to enter personal information. We may use standard server logs and basic analytics to see which pages are visited. We do not sell personal information.

Sharing

We share the details of a service request with the plumber who will do the work and with the people who help us run the service. We do not sell your information to third parties.

Your choices

You can ask us not to contact you again, and you can ask what information we hold about a call. Email contact@247plumbercupertino.com or call (650) 860-1670.

Changes

We may update this policy, and the current version is always on this page.
