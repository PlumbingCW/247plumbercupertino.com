A burst pipe puts gallons per minute into walls and floors, so emergency burst pipe repair in Cupertino, CA starts with stopping the water. Our 24/7 line is open now. Shut off the main water valve, open the lowest faucet to drain the lines, switch off the electricity to any wet area at the breaker, and move belongings and rugs out of the water path.

A burst pipe repair replaces or fixes the failed section, restores the supply, and checks the surrounding pipe for the same weakness. It is a leak that has turned into a failure, and the repair is straightforward once we reach the line.

Shut off the water first

  1. 1

If you cannot find the main, the meter near the street has a shutoff, and the water provider can help. A key may be needed. We can talk you through it on the phone.

What causes a pipe to burst in a mild climate

Hard freezes are less common in the Santa Clara Valley than in colder regions, so most bursts come from other causes. High water pressure stresses old joints, corrosion thins pipe walls from the inside, and a poorly made fitting can let go years later. Earth movement can shear a line, and an old supply valve can fail without warning.

We check the water pressure at your hose bib, and a regulator that is set too high gets adjusted. A burst is often a symptom of an aging line, and we say so when we see the pattern.

Supply valve and flex line breaks

Many floods start at a broken supply valve under a sink or toilet, or a braided flex line behind a washing machine. Those are quick repairs once the water is off. The valve behind a toilet or the angle stop under a faucet can be replaced in minutes, and a new connector prevents a repeat.

If a valve will not shut off fully, or a handle breaks when you turn it, call even if the leak looks small. Forcing it can make the break larger.

Cracked drain pipes are different

A cracked drain or waste pipe leaks wastewater instead of supply water. The repair is a different one, and a camera or a cleanout can show the crack. Drain pipe repair may involve cast iron, ABS or PVC, and the method depends on access.

If you see wet drywall below a bathroom, or water that appears only when a fixture is used, tell dispatch. That points to a drain leak, and we bring the right parts.

Replacement pipe and fittings have to meet the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and a larger repair may need a permit.

Water damage and next steps

After the pipe is repaired, wet drywall and insulation need to dry. Remove standing water, run fans and a dehumidifier, and photograph everything for your claim. We can recommend next steps, and a restoration specialist handles drying and mold prevention. We repair the pipe and check for other weak points.

The Cupertino Building Division says replacing a defective concealed pipe needs a permit, while stopping a leak generally does not. We tell you which applies before the repair. A bigger replacement may call for repiping.

What affects burst pipe repair pricing

Access, the pipe material, the number of failed sections, the time of day and whether a permit is needed set the price. A single accessible break costs less than a long run behind a finished wall. We give a clear price before work starts.
