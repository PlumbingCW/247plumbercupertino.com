Sunnyvale, plumbing and the housing that came after the orchards

Sunnyvale was first settled as the town of Encinal in 1897, took the Sunnyvale name in 1901 and incorporated in 1912. Orchards filled the valley for decades, and after World War II the orchards and farms were cleared for homes, factories and offices. That history matters because homes built in that postwar wave are now many decades old, and supply lines, water heaters and sewer laterals from those years are reaching the end of their lives.

If your Sunnyvale house is of that era, ask a plumber to inspect the supply lines, the water heater and the sewer lateral before a leak forces the issue. Our leak detection and camera inspection visits can be scheduled ahead of a problem.

Eichler homes and slab leaks in Sunnyvale

The southern half of Sunnyvale is largely residential and includes 16 tracts with more than 1,100 Eichler homes. Eichler homes are known for radiant heating pipes laid in the concrete slab, and slab-on-grade construction means supply and heating pipes can leak under the floor.

A warm patch on the floor, a heating system that has lost output, or a water bill that jumped without explanation can point to a slab or radiant leak. Tell dispatch your home is an Eichler or slab-on-grade design when you call, because slab leak repair needs the right locating tools and the right repair plan for each system.

Before we arrive: if you have a burst pipe or active leak

1

Close the main valve.

Find the main water shutoff where the line enters the house and close it. Open the lowest faucet to drain the lines.

2

Cut power to wet rooms.

Switch off the breaker for any room with water running or standing. Keep clear of outlets.

Move rugs, furniture and electronics away from the water path, and keep children and pets out of wet rooms.

Sunnyvale water: where it comes from and what to test

The City of Sunnyvale lists several sources. They are the San Francisco Public Utilities Commission, the Santa Clara Valley Water District, California Water Service for a small part of the city, recycled water and Sunnyvale wells. The city also says only the area north of El Camino Real receives fluoridated water from the San Francisco Public Utilities Commission.

The city page does not give a hardness number, so test your tap or ask the utility for its water quality report. Hardness affects tankless heaters, which need descaling, and it shows up as scale on fixtures. See tankless service and water softener and filtration.

Permits and licensing for Sunnyvale work

Permit rules in Sunnyvale are set by the City of Sunnyvale, not by Cupertino, so ask its building division what applies to your job. We handle permit paperwork for the work we do.
