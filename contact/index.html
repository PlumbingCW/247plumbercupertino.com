Call dispatch

The fastest way to reach a plumber is to call (650) 860-1670. The line is open 24 hours a day, every day, including holidays. Tell the dispatcher what is happening, where you are and whether water is reaching the floor, and a licensed plumber is sent the same day when one is available.

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call us.

Our office

Cupertino 24/7 Plumbing, 19626 Stevens Creek Blvd, Cupertino, CA 95014. Email: contact@247plumbercupertino.com. We take service requests by phone so that we can ask the right questions while you are standing in front of the problem.

What to have ready

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • What is happening and where the water is.
  • Whether you shut off the main valve.
  • The age and fuel type if it is a water heater.
  • A photo of the problem, if it is safe to take one.

Emergency or planned work

Emergencies go first, so call at once for leaks, backups and failed water heaters. For planned work, such as a repipe, a remodel or a heater upgrade, call and tell the dispatcher you are planning ahead so the right visit is scheduled.

Tell us about access too. Gates, pets, parking and building rules matter, and a quick mention saves time on arrival.

Every plumber we send works to the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).
