Kitchen Sink Plumbing Rough In: A Cupertino Remodel Guide

The rough in is the plumbing hidden in the wall before the cabinets and the sink go in. Get the heights and the vent right now and the finish work is easy.

By the Cupertino 24/7 Plumbing team. Published November 16, 2026. Updated November 16, 2026.

A kitchen sink rough in is the set of supply lines, the drain and the vent that sit inside the wall and floor before the finish work begins. After the drywall is up and the cabinets are set, none of this is easy to change. That is why a remodel plan should settle the sink location, the sink type and the appliances before the plumber starts.

If your project involves a new faucet at the end, our faucet repair page covers fixture issues. This guide focuses on the pipes behind the wall.

Know the parts of the rough in

Hot and cold supply stubs

Two supply lines come out of the wall below the sink, each with a shutoff valve for the faucet.

Drain stub

A drain pipe leaves the sink trap and runs into the wall or floor to the main drain.

Vent

A vent pipe lets air into the drain so water flows freely and the trap keeps its seal.

Appliance connections

The dishwasher and disposal need a supply, a drain connection and power.

Heights and distances

Exact heights depend on the sink and the cabinet, and the plumber works from the sink and the cabinet drawings. The drain usually sits low in the wall, and the supply stubs sit above it. The trap has to line up with the drain so the water drops cleanly.

Do not guess. Bring the sink spec sheet and the cabinet plan to the plumber. A few inches off can force an awkward trap or a cut cabinet back.

Why venting matters

A drain without a proper vent gurgles and drains slowly, and the trap can lose its water seal and let sewer gas into the kitchen. The vent can be a pipe that runs up through the wall, or an air admittance valve where code allows it. Code decides which option you can use.

An island sink often has no wall behind it, so venting needs a special design. If your remodel includes an island sink, raise it at the start of planning.

Dishwasher and disposal

The dishwasher drain usually connects to the disposal inlet or to the sink drain above the trap through a high loop. The disposal needs a nearby outlet or a hard wired circuit. Decide on both before the rough in, because the drain layout depends on them.

If you plan to add a disposal, see the notes in garbage disposal repair on fit and power.

Pipe materials

Supply lines are commonly copper or PEX, and drains are commonly plastic. Each material has its own rules for support, joints and transitions. A plumber chooses based on the house, the budget and the local code. Mixing metals without the right fittings can cause corrosion.

If your home has older galvanized or corroded pipes, a remodel is the best time to fix the whole run. Our repiping page explains when that makes sense.

Sequence for a clean remodel

  1. 1

    Plan the layout.

    Pick the sink, the faucet, the appliances and the cabinet layout before any plumbing is cut.

  2. 2

    Open the wall.

    Expose the old supply and drain, and check their condition before building on them.

  3. 3

    Rough in the pipes.

    Set supply stubs, drain and vent to the heights on the plan, and pressure test the supply.

  4. 4

    Inspect.

    Call for the permit inspection while the pipes are still visible, before the wall closes.

  5. 5

    Close and finish.

    Close the wall, set the cabinets and counters, then install the sink, faucet and appliances.

Drain and vent sizing follow the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and the inspector checks the rough in before the wall closes.

Cupertino rules and kitchen remodels

A kitchen remodel that moves plumbing is permitted work, and the City of Cupertino Building Division handles plumbing permits. The inspection happens before the wall is closed, so schedule it with your contractor. Do not close the wall early to save time, because the inspector has to see the pipes.

Cupertino sewer service is provided by a sanitary district, and many homes also have older laterals between the house and the street. If you are opening the kitchen and the drain looks old, a camera inspection of the lateral is a smart add on. For neighbors in Sunnyvale, our Sunnyvale page has the local details.
