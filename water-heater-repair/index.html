No hot water: the usual causes

A water heater has a short list of failure points, and a technician with a meter finds the cause quickly. Gas tank units fail at the thermocouple, the gas control valve, or the pilot assembly. Electric tanks fail at the upper or lower heating element or a thermostat. Both fail when sediment builds up on the bottom of the tank and the burner or element can no longer heat through it.

Hybrid heat pump models add a compressor, fan and condensate drain that can fail separately from the tank. We repair the parts that are repairable and explain when the tank itself is the problem.

Water only lukewarm

A failed lower element or a worn thermostat can leave only the top of the tank hot.

Pilot light keeps going out

A worn thermocouple or a draft in the closet is the common cause.

Popping or rumbling sounds

Sediment trapped under the burner boils and thumps, a sign of buildup.

Water around the base

A tank leak is a replacement call, while a loose fitting or relief valve can be a simple repair.

Water heater leaks: repair or replace

Leaks are the biggest cluster in what callers describe, so we sort them by source. A drip at the pipe connections or at the temperature and pressure relief valve can be repaired. A leak from the tank seam or the bottom of the tank cannot, because the steel liner has corroded through.

When the answer is replacement, the decision is in water heater replacement, where we talk through tank versus tankless and what Cupertino rules apply. A leaking tank that is still pressurized can fail further, so shutting off the cold water inlet is the first step.

Do not touch the relief valve line

The relief valve discharge pipe is a safety device. Do not cap it, plug it, or tighten a fitting on it. If water drips from it, call the line and describe the amount and the temperature of the water.

Seismic strapping and code in Cupertino

California law requires all new and replacement water heaters, and existing residential water heaters, to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquake motion. If a repair visit shows a tank standing with no straps, we point it out, because a heater that falls in an earthquake can rupture the gas or water line.

The Cupertino Building Division lists a water heater replacement for standard gas tanks as an instant online permit with conditions, and the standard permit applies to other replacements. A repair of a part does not need that permit, while a replacement does.

Hybrid, electric and gas models

Gas units are common in older homes near De Anza Boulevard and Stevens Creek Boulevard, while newer homes and townhomes more often have electric or heat pump units. The Bay Area Air District has a zero-emission rule for residential gas water heaters that applies at replacement, which is another reason to ask about hybrid heat pump models early. The district has moved to push the compliance date to 2028, so check its building appliances page before you buy.

Repair of a hybrid heat pump heater is a different skill from tank repair, and we say so up front if a unit needs the manufacturer's parts channel.
