When a restaurant drain backs up during service or a leak floods a retail floor, the cost of waiting is lost business. Our 24/7 line takes calls for emergency commercial plumbing in Cupertino, CA. While you wait, close the main water valve if water is flowing, stop using fixtures on the affected line, and keep customers and staff clear of wet floors and any wet electrical equipment.

Commercial plumbing service covers the same repairs as residential work, on larger systems: drains and main lines, water heaters, supply leaks, fixtures, grease interceptors, backflow assemblies and storm drains.

Restaurants and food service

Kitchens put grease, food solids and heat into drain lines, and the lines clog faster than residential ones. A floor drain that backs up, a three-compartment sink that drains slowly, or a smell from a floor drain are early warnings. We clear the line, and hydro jetting removes the grease film that a cable leaves behind.

Grease traps and interceptors need regular pumping and cleaning, and we coordinate service so the schedule matches your volume. A full or neglected interceptor is a common source of backups.

Offices, retail and multi-unit properties

Properties along corridors like Stevens Creek Boulevard include shops, offices and multi-unit buildings, and each has its own set of risks. Restrooms with several fixtures on one stack can clog together. Tenant spaces share risers and drains, and a leak in one unit shows up in another.

We coordinate with property managers and tenants to isolate the problem and limit shutoffs to the affected section. Clear communication matters when several tenants share a building.

Backflow testing and prevention

Backflow preventers protect the public water supply from contamination, and many commercial properties need them tested on a schedule set by the water provider. San Jose Water and California Water Service serve Cupertino, and the provider for your site tells you what applies. A failed test means a repair or replacement of the assembly, and a retest.

We test and repair common assemblies, and we explain what the provider requires. A device that has failed needs to be fixed promptly, and we keep records of results.

Leak detection and water loss

A leak in a commercial building can be hard to find because the plumbing is large and partly hidden. A water meter that moves when the building is closed is the first sign. We isolate sections, use acoustic and thermal tools, and trace the leak, which is described in leak detection.

We are honest about scope. Large systems, such as fire mains and multi-story risers, may need a specialist, and we say so rather than overpromise.

Storm drains, grease and cast iron

Storm drains clog with leaves and debris before the rainy season and need to flow freely. Old cast iron pipe can crack and corrode, and we assess it with a camera before repair. We handle drain cleaning for these systems as part of commercial service.

Commercial work follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), including backflow protection, and grease interceptor rules are set by the local agency.

What to do before we arrive

Shut off the water to the affected area and keep customers and staff away from wet floors. Move stock and electronics out of the water path. Note which fixtures are affected and when the problem began, then call the 24/7 line.
