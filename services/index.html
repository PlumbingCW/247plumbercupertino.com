Emergency repairs

These calls cannot wait, and dispatch answers 24/7. A clog that keeps the house from working goes to drain cleaning, and a line packed with grease goes to hydro jetting. A main line that is backing up is handled through sewer line cleaning, and when the pipe itself is damaged it becomes sewer line repair.

Water that will not stop is a burst pipe call, and water you cannot find is leak detection. A hidden leak under concrete is a slab leak. A failed tank or a heater with no hot water is water heater repair or water heater replacement.

Fixtures and kitchens

A running or overflowing toilet is a toilet repair visit, a dripping or stuck valve is a faucet repair, and a jammed or leaking disposal is a garbage disposal repair. These are usually quick visits and the price is clear before we start.

Planned work and inspections

When you can plan, we schedule. A new unit goes through water heater installation or tankless service. Failing supply lines are a repiping project, and remodel and addition plumbing is plumbing installation. A look inside the sewer line before you buy or dig is a sewer camera inspection.

Hard water, spotted glass and scale in a heater start with a test of your tap, then water softener and filtration if the result calls for it.

Business plumbing

Restaurants, offices, retail and multi-unit buildings use commercial plumbing for after-hours repairs, grease lines, backflow testing and leak detection.

Where we work

We serve all of Cupertino, and our Sunnyvale page covers the neighboring city. See the service area for Santa Clara, Saratoga and Los Altos.

How to choose

If water is moving where it should not, start with the emergency list. If a fixture works poorly but nothing is flooding, a fixture visit is enough. If you are planning, call early so the permit and the parts line up.

Whatever the job, you get a clear price before work starts, and a plumber explains the problem in plain language.

Every service listed here follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC).
