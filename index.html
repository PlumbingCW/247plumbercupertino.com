Before we arrive: what to do in a plumbing emergency

1

Shut off the water.

Find the main valve where the supply line enters the house, often near the water heater or an exterior wall, and close it. Open the lowest faucet to drain the lines.

2

Kill power to wet areas.

Switch off the breaker for any room with standing water or water running down a wall. Do not touch electrical items while standing in water.

3

Stop using drains.

For a backup, do not run the dishwasher, washer or showers. Everything you add goes to the lowest open drain.

4

Call the 24/7 line.

Tell dispatch what you see and where the water is coming from. Photograph the damage for your insurance claim.

Licensed plumbers and how to check

We dispatch licensed and insured plumbers. In California, contractors hired for work that costs one thousand dollars or more in labor and materials combined must be licensed by the Contractors State License Board, and you can check any contractor on the board's License Check page. The board advises paying no more than ten percent down or one thousand dollars, whichever is less. We follow those rules and suggest you apply them to any plumber you hire.

For permits, check with the City of Cupertino Building Division, which issues plumbing permits for jobs such as water heater replacement. Plumbing in California follows the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). We handle the paperwork with you.
