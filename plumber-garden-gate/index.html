Backup in Garden Gate?

Stop running water, then call dispatch and say which fixtures are slow.

Try first

  • Use a plunger on a single clogged toilet or sink.
  • Check that the stopper or strainer is clear.
  • Run hot water to see if the drain moves.

Call a plumber if

  • More than one fixture is slow or gurgling.
  • The clog returns within days.
  • Water or sewage comes up in a tub or floor drain.

Single-family homes, duplexes and apartments

If you live in a duplex or apartment, call your landlord or property manager first, since they usually decide who repairs shared lines. If you own a unit or a house, we can come directly.

Tell the dispatcher how many units share the line. A backup that affects the unit next door usually means the blockage is downstream of both.

Everyday calls

Clogged drains

Slow sinks and tubs are handled with drain cleaning. A plunger clears simple clogs, and a machine handles the rest.

Toilets

Running, weak or leaking toilets are covered under toilet repair. A repeat clog may point to the drain and not the bowl.

Hidden leaks

A damp wall, a stain on a ceiling or a bill that jumped can mean a hidden leak. Leak detection finds it with tools first.

Giving good directions

Garden Gate sits between Interstate 280 and the Heart of the City area, so streets are reached from De Anza Boulevard, Mary Avenue and Stevens Creek Boulevard. Give the dispatcher your street, the nearest cross street and a description of the entrance. If you live in a complex, include the building and unit.

A call that includes the symptom, the number of fixtures affected and the location of the main shutoff gets a plumber moving with the right tools. If someone can meet the plumber at the door, say so, and mention any pets or locked gates.
