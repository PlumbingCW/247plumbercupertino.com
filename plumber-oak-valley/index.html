Where Oak Valley is

Oak Valley is the northwest corner of Cupertino. The City of Cupertino General Plan bounds it by Interstate 280, Los Altos, Foothill Boulevard, Stevens Creek Boulevard and county open space. Gate of Heaven Cemetery and The Forum senior living community are inside it.

We take calls from every street here. Tell dispatch the street name and what is wrong, and a plumber is sent when one is available.

A newer neighborhood by local standards

The General Plan describes Oak Valley as mostly detached single-family homes from the late 1990s and early 2000s, west of the railroad tracks. Next to the older tracts in central and southeastern Cupertino, that is a more recent group of houses.

Newer does not mean problem free, and the plan does not say what pipe is in these homes. Plumbing parts wear out whatever the age of the house, so each call starts with a look at the actual system.

Water heaters in a house of this era

This is general plumbing information. A house built in the late 1990s or early 2000s may be on a second water heater by now, because tank heaters have a limited life. A puddle at the base, rusty hot water or a pilot that will not stay lit are common reasons to call.

We cover water heater repair for fixable problems and replacement when the tank has failed. A replacement needs a permit from the City of Cupertino Building Division.

Tankless, filtration and faucets

Some owners choose a tankless unit when a tank is due for replacement. Tankless water heater service covers installs and the yearly descaling that keeps them running well.

If you notice spots on glass or a film on fixtures, test the tap before buying equipment. Water softener and filtration starts with a test of your own water and not a guess about the neighborhood. A leaking or stiff faucet is a quick visit, see faucet repair.

Call the same day if

  • Water is spreading on the floor.
  • The heater is leaking at the tank.
  • Hot water is rusty or brown.

Book a regular visit if

  • The faucet drips slowly.
  • Water pressure has dropped over time.
  • You want a tankless quote.

If the water heater is leaking now

1

Cut the fuel.

Turn off the electric breaker or set a gas unit to pilot or off, using the control on the unit.

2

Close the cold valve.

The cold water valve on top of the tank stops new water from feeding the leak.

3

Contain the water.

Move items away, mop standing water and, if you can, run a hose from the drain valve to a floor drain or outdoors.

4

Call dispatch.

Say whether the unit is in a garage, closet or attic so the plumber can plan access.
