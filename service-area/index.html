Cupertino

Our home base is Cupertino, in Santa Clara County. The main ZIP code is 95014. We serve all of the city, including Monta Vista, Rancho Rinconada, the City Center area, the Vallco site, De Anza College and the areas near Apple Park.

Roads that matter for dispatch include Stevens Creek Boulevard, De Anza Boulevard, the Lawrence Expressway side of the city and the I-280 and SR 85 interchange. Creeks including Permanente Creek and Stevens Creek run through or near the city, which is worth knowing if you are near the water when a sewer lateral problem repeats.

Sunnyvale

Sunnyvale borders Cupertino and has its own page. We cover ZIP codes 94087, 94086, 94085 and 94089. See emergency plumbing in Sunnyvale for housing and plumbing notes, including Eichler homes and slab leaks.

Santa Clara, Saratoga and Los Altos

We also serve Santa Clara, where the ZIP codes include 95050 and 95051, Saratoga, where they include 95070, and Los Altos, where they include 94022 and 94024. These communities do not have their own pages yet, and the same 24/7 dispatch covers them. Call and give us your address.

What we handle across the area

The most common calls are drain cleaning, water heater repair and leak detection. The full list is on the services page, and planned jobs include repiping and camera inspections.

How dispatch works across towns

All calls go to one 24/7 line, so you do not need to find a different number for a different city. The dispatcher confirms your address, matches the job to the right plumber and sends them by the best route.

If you are close to a boundary, such as the Cupertino and Sunnyvale line, call anyway. We would rather confirm coverage than have you wait on a busy line elsewhere.

Santa Clara

Santa Clara sits east of Cupertino and Sunnyvale and includes ZIP codes such as 95050 and 95051. Homes near the Mission City downtown are older, and older homes mean older supply lines and sewer laterals. A camera inspection before a remodel is a sensible step there.

Businesses near the big stadium and office districts use our commercial plumbing service for after-hours leaks and backups.

Saratoga

Saratoga lies south and west of Cupertino, with ZIP code 95070 covering most of the town. Downtown Saratoga along Big Basin Way has older commercial buildings, and the residential streets stretch up toward the hills. Hillside lots often have long sewer laterals and varying water pressure.

If your Saratoga home has low or uneven pressure, tell the dispatcher, because a pressure test and a look at the regulator are often the first steps.

Los Altos

Los Altos borders Cupertino to the northwest and uses ZIP codes 94022 and 94024. The downtown area along Main Street mixes shops, restaurants and older homes. Restaurants rely on clean grease lines and working floor drains, and a quick call to dispatch gets a plumber started on a backup.

Residential calls here lean toward water heater failures, slab leaks and older sewer laterals with roots.

What coverage means

We cover a city when dispatch can send a licensed plumber the same day when one is available. During busy weather or holidays, availability changes, and dispatch tells you honestly what is possible. We do not promise a specific arrival time.

For any address, the dispatcher confirms the cross street and the ZIP code first. That keeps the right truck headed to the right door.

Tell the dispatcher about anything that affects access, such as a gated community, a narrow driveway or a hillside lot. Those details help the plumber bring the right tools on the first trip. They also help us tell you honestly whether the job is a quick visit or needs a second trip.

Each city building department enforces the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), so permit steps can differ by city even though the code is shared.
