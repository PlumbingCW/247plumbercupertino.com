A leak in the pipes under a concrete slab is a hidden problem that can damage flooring and the slab itself, so emergency slab leak repair in Cupertino, CA is a call to make early. Our 24/7 line takes it. While you wait, shut off the main water valve if you are sure of the leak, keep off a wet or warm floor, and photograph any damage.

A slab leak is a break in a water line that runs under or through the concrete foundation. Hot water lines under a slab are common culprits, because heat and movement stress the pipe, and a hot leak warms the floor above.

Signs of a slab leak

Warm spot on the floor

A hot water line leaking under the slab can warm a patch of floor.

Sound of running water

A hiss with every fixture off suggests a pressurized leak under the floor.

Water bill jump

A slab leak can run constantly and add to the bill.

Cracks or lifted flooring

Water softens the base under tile and wood, and floors can lift or buckle.

How we locate it

We start by confirming a leak with a meter check and a pressure test of the lines. Then we isolate the hot and cold lines to see which is losing pressure. Acoustic listening and thermal imaging narrow the leak to a few inches, and marking the floor lets us open the smallest possible area.

Pinpointing matters because breaking a slab in the wrong spot costs time and money and creates a second repair. A good locator pass can save an entire section of flooring.

A single leak in a line with a history of pinholes usually returns. We tell you if your pipe pattern points toward repiping, and we do not push a larger job when a spot repair will last.

Why the leak is urgent

Water under a slab saturates the soil and base material, and it can undermine flooring, raise humidity, and in some cases affect the foundation. Even a slow leak can turn into a large repair if ignored.

Eichler and radiant slab homes in the area

Southern Sunnyvale has more than 1,100 Eichler homes in 16 tracts, and Eichler homes are known for radiant heating pipes embedded in the concrete slab. Those pipes can leak, and the symptom is a damp or warm patch and a drop in heating performance. If your home is an Eichler or a similar slab-on-grade design, tell us when you call so the right tools come with us. See our Sunnyvale page.

Cupertino homeowners can ask the same question of any older slab-on-grade house, and our process applies to domestic water lines and, with the right specialist, radiant lines too.

Repairs to supply lines under a slab fall under the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and the work may need a permit.

What to do before we arrive

If you hear water running with everything off or feel a warm spot on the floor, close the main shutoff and keep the family off the area. Switch off power to wet rooms at the breaker, and photograph the floor for your insurance claim. Then call the 24/7 line.

Insurance and documentation

Many homeowner policies cover water damage from a sudden leak and may cover the cost of access to the leak, though they often exclude the pipe repair itself. We photograph the leak, the location and the damage, and we write a plain description of the findings. Ask your agent what your policy covers before the work starts.

What affects slab leak repair pricing

The method, the number of leaks, the flooring above, the depth of the line and the time of day set the number. A locate and spot repair is quoted separately from a reroute or repipe. We give a clear price before work starts.
