What we do

Cupertino 24/7 Plumbing is a dispatch line for plumbing service in Cupertino, CA and the surrounding South Bay. You call, a dispatcher listens, and a licensed and insured plumber is sent the same day when one is available. We handle emergencies at any hour and schedule planned work such as water heater installs, repipes and camera inspections.

Our office is at 19626 Stevens Creek Blvd, Cupertino, CA 95014. Our phone is (650) 860-1670, and our email is contact@247plumbercupertino.com.

How we work

You get a clear price before work starts, and no work begins until you agree to it. We keep repair and diagnosis separate so you can compare. If a job is outside what our plumbers handle, such as fire mains, we say so.

We do not make promises we cannot keep. We do not quote an arrival time, because traffic and earlier calls change what is possible, and we tell you what is available when you call.

How to verify a plumbing license

In California, contractors hired for work that costs one thousand dollars or more in labor and materials combined must be licensed by the Contractors State License Board. The board has a License Check page on its website and a phone line, and it advises paying no more than ten percent down or one thousand dollars, whichever is less.

Check any plumber you hire, including us.

What we handle

Start with drain cleaning, water heater repair and leak detection, which are the most requested services. The full list is on our services page.

What to expect when you call

A dispatcher answers, asks about the problem and your address, and tells you what is available. If water is running, they walk you through shutting off the main. A licensed plumber is then sent the same day when one is available.

On arrival, the plumber looks at the problem, explains what is wrong and gives you a clear price before work starts. If you decline, you owe nothing for the quote.

What we will not do

We do not publish made up reviews, star ratings or customer counts, because none of that helps you fix a leak. We do not promise an arrival time we cannot control. We do not quote a firm number for a problem nobody has seen.

We also do not push work you do not need. If a repair solves the problem, we say so. If a replacement is the honest answer, we explain why and show you what we found.

How we choose the work we take

Plumbing covers a lot of ground, and we keep to the work our plumbers do well every day. That is drains, sewer lines, water heaters, leaks, supply lines, fixtures and light commercial plumbing. Fire sprinkler systems, large mains and specialty process piping are outside our scope, and we say so rather than guess.

If your project needs another trade, such as an electrician for a new circuit or a roofer for a roof leak, we tell you early. A clear scope at the start prevents surprises later.

What to do before the plumber arrives

  • Clear the area around the fixture or the water heater.
  • Know where your main water shutoff is.
  • Secure pets and keep children away from the work area.
  • Photograph any damage for your insurance claim.
  • Write down when the problem started and what changed.

A few minutes of preparation shortens the visit, and it helps the plumber find the cause faster.

Plumbing work in California is governed by the California Plumbing Code, which is based on the Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and local building departments enforce it through permits and inspections.
