The Fairgrove Eichler tract

Fairgrove is a residential tract in southeastern Cupertino. The City of Cupertino General Plan puts it between Phil Lane on the north, Tantau Avenue on the east, Bollinger Road on the south and Miller Avenue on the west. Hyde Middle School is in the tract.

The city zones Fairgrove R1e, Eichler Single Family, and describes Eichler homes built in the early 1960s. Counts differ by source, from about 220 to 229 homes, so treat the number as approximate.

What Eichler homes mean for your plumbing

This is general information about the Eichler design, not a finding about any one house. Eichler interiors are documented as having concrete slab floors with integral radiant heating. Slab-on-grade construction puts water lines and any heating loops below the floor instead of in a crawlspace.

Whether your house still has working radiant heat, or what material the heating pipes are, only an inspection can tell. We do not assume it from the tract.

Signs worth a call

A warm patch on the floor, a heating system that lost output, the sound of running water when everything is off, or a water bill that jumped without a reason.

Any of these can come from many causes. Leak detection uses listening and thermal tools to find water without cutting into the floor first, and slab leak repair explains what repair options exist once a leak is located.

Leak in a Fairgrove Eichler?

Close the main valve if water is running, then call dispatch and say your home is an Eichler.

Before the plumber arrives

1

Close the main.

If water is running, shut the main valve where the supply line enters the house and open a low faucet to drain the lines.

2

Look and listen.

Note any warm spots on the floor, wet baseboards or the sound of water when every fixture is off.

3

Read the meter.

Write down the meter reading, wait an hour with no water in use, and read it again. A change means water is going somewhere.

4

Tell dispatch about the house.

Say it is an Eichler or a slab home, and mention any repairs or a repipe you know about.

Photos of any damage help with an insurance claim later. Moving rugs and furniture off a wet floor also stops stains from spreading while you wait. If the water is near an outlet or appliance, leave the breaker for that room off until a plumber has looked.

Drains, heaters and sewer lines

Flat roofs, slab floors and open interiors do not change how a drain clogs. A slow sink or a backed up tub is still handled with drain cleaning, and a sewer camera inspection shows the line when clearing it does not hold.

A tank that leaks or stops heating needs water heater repair or a replacement. A replacement requires a permit from the City of Cupertino Building Division, and the work follows the California Plumbing Code.
