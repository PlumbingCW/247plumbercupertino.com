A tankless water heater heats water on demand instead of storing it. For homeowners in Cupertino, CA we install, repair and flush tankless units, and we schedule these visits through the 24/7 line. A well-maintained unit gives steady hot water, but it needs a flush at intervals and a proper gas line, vent and drain.

Tankless service splits into three jobs: installation or conversion from a tank, repair of error codes and component failures, and descaling flush. Each needs a slightly different set of tools and skills.

Descale and flush: why it matters

Mineral scale builds inside the heat exchanger and slows flow. The water supply in Cupertino varies by provider and source, and San Jose Water's published report lists average hardness of about 99 parts per million for treated imported surface water and about 288 for groundwater. Your tap depends on the blend, so ask your provider for its water quality report or test your tap.

A flush circulates a descaling solution through the heat exchanger using a pump and hoses, and then rinses with clean water. Manufacturers call for flushes at intervals set by water hardness and use, and we follow the schedule for your unit. Skipping it is a leading cause of error codes and reduced flow.

Error code on the display

Codes often point to scale, a venting issue or a flame sensor. We read the code and test the part.

Hot water runs cold then hot

Scale, a low flow rate or a failing sensor can cause this swing.

Reduced flow

A clogged inlet filter or scaled heat exchanger cuts the output.

Unit shuts off mid-shower

Flame failure, venting or gas supply may be the cause.

Installing or converting to tankless

Replacing a tank with a tankless unit is not a swap. Tankless gas units draw a lot of gas, so the gas line often needs resizing. They need special venting, a condensate drain for condensing models, and a power outlet. Electric tankless units draw high amperage and may need panel work.

The Cupertino Building Division says its instant online permit for water heater replacement does not apply to tankless units, so a tankless installation goes through the standard permit process. We handle the paperwork and the inspection, and the state requirement for strapping applies where a tank is present.

Which homes suit tankless

Households with high hot water demand and limited space often benefit, and so do homeowners who want to stop heating water they are not using. It is less suited to homes where gas or electric capacity is expensive to upgrade. We help you decide by checking your gas meter, line size and venting path.

Homes near Vallco, the City Center and the Marketplace include townhomes and condos where HOA rules may govern venting locations. Ask your HOA before you commit to a location.

Repair and error codes

Common repair items include flame sensors, igniters, flow sensors, inlet filters, temperature thermistors and condensate drains. We diagnose with a meter and the manufacturer's service data. If a unit is out of warranty and the heat exchanger has failed, replacement may cost less than a repair, and we say so.

A unit that loses hot water on a cold morning is covered by our emergency water heater pages, such as water heater repair.

A flush depends on the unit and how scaled it is. An install depends on gas line size, venting, electrical, permit and access. We give a clear price before work starts.
