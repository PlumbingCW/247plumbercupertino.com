A faucet that will not shut off, a supply line that has burst under a sink, or a shower valve that drips onto a bathroom floor can all turn into a flood. Our line for emergency faucet repair in Cupertino, CA answers 24/7. While you wait, close the shutoff valve under the sink, or turn off the water to the whole house if the valve is stuck or leaking.

Faucet and sink repair covers the faucet itself, its cartridge or washers, the supply lines and shutoff valves, the sink drain and trap, and shower and tub valves. We repair when parts are available and replace when a fixture is worn out.

Dripping and leaking faucets

A drip comes from a worn washer, seat, O-ring or cartridge, and the part depends on the faucet design. Compression faucets use washers. Cartridge and ball faucets use cartridges. Ceramic disc faucets use a disc cartridge. A drip wastes water and raises the bill, and mineral buildup makes it worse.

We identify the faucet, source the right cartridge and replace it. If the faucet body is corroded or the finish is failing, replacement is the better value. We tell you which applies.

Drip from the spout

Worn cartridge, washer or seat is the usual cause.

Leak at the handle

A worn O-ring or packing nut lets water escape.

Water under the sink

A loose supply line, a bad shutoff valve or a leaking drain.

Low flow

A clogged aerator or a failing cartridge.

Supply valves and flex lines that fail

A broken or stuck shutoff valve under a sink or behind a toilet is a common reason for a flood. Old angle stops seize, and a braided connector can burst. Replacing the valve and the connector takes a short visit, and it restores your ability to shut the water off at the fixture.

If a valve will not turn or leaks when you touch it, call. Forcing it can crack the valve body. We replace the valve and test the whole connection.

Shower and tub valve problems

A shower that will not shut off, a tub spout that drips, or a handle that is hard to turn usually points to a worn cartridge in the shower valve. Some valves need a trim kit change, and some need the valve body replaced, which means opening the wall.

A showerhead that leaks at the pipe connection may only need a new seal and thread tape. We check before we recommend opening any wall. A leak behind the shower wall is a more serious problem, and leak detection can find it.

Sink drains and traps

A leak under a sink often comes from the drain trap or a slip nut that has loosened. The fix may be tightening or replacing a washer, or replacing the trap. A sink that drains slowly may have a clog, which is covered under drain cleaning.

When to replace the fixture

A faucet that has been repaired more than once, has a corroded body, or is discontinued may not be worth another repair. A replacement can be set the same day, and we install the faucet you choose or recommend one. Replacing a sink or a faucet falls under fixture work that the Cupertino Building Division lists for permits, and we confirm what applies to your job.

What affects fixture repair pricing

The fixture, the part, access and the time of day set the price. A cartridge swap is a small job, and a shower valve replacement is larger. We give a clear price before work starts.
