A toilet that overflows, leaks at the base or runs all night needs prompt attention, so our line for emergency toilet repair in Cupertino, CA is open 24/7. If the bowl is rising, take the tank lid off, lift the float or flapper arm and turn the supply valve on the wall behind the toilet clockwise. That stops the refill while you wait.

Toilet repair covers the parts inside the tank, the seal at the floor, the supply line and valve, and the clog in the trap or drain. Toilet installation covers a new unit, a replacement, or a smart toilet with a bidet seat that needs power and a water supply.

How to tell if the toilet is clogged

A clogged toilet flushes slowly, the water rises to the rim and drains with a gurgle, or the bowl fills and does not empty at all. A partial clog may clear with a plunger. A complete clog, or one that comes back, may be in the trap or in the line past the toilet.

If other fixtures also drain slowly, the problem is probably in the drain line and not in the toilet. In that case, see drain cleaning, because plunging a toilet will not clear a main line problem.

Running toilet and leaks inside the tank

A toilet that runs constantly wastes water and raises the bill. The cause is usually a worn flapper, a bent chain, a fill valve that does not shut off, or a water level set too high. We replace the worn parts and set the level so the toilet stops running.

A quick test: put a few drops of food coloring in the tank and wait a few minutes. If color appears in the bowl without flushing, the flapper is leaking. A running toilet can add a lot to the bill, which is why a meter that moves is worth chasing.

Leaks at the base and loose toilets

Water around the base of a toilet usually means a failed wax ring or a loose connection to the floor flange. A toilet that rocks, or a damp floor beside it, points the same direction. Left alone, the subfloor can soften and rot.

The repair means pulling the toilet, replacing the wax ring or seal, checking the flange, and resetting the toilet. If the flange is cracked or low, we repair it so the new seal can hold.

Never ignore a leak at the base

Even a small leak can soak the subfloor below a bathroom. If you see a stain on the ceiling underneath, shut off the supply valve and call. See leak detection if you are unsure where the water goes.

Toilet installation and smart toilets

A new toilet is a set of simple steps done carefully: shut off water, remove the old unit, clean and inspect the flange, set a new seal, bolt and level the toilet, connect the supply, and test. A smart toilet adds a power outlet near the toilet and a water connection for the bidet function, and we coordinate the outlet with an electrician when needed.

Replacing a toilet is plumbing work, and the Cupertino Building Division lists plumbing permits for replacement of fixtures including water closets. We confirm what applies to your job.

What to do before we arrive

Shut off the supply valve behind the toilet, mop up standing water, and keep the bathroom door closed to stop kids and pets. If wastewater is involved, avoid contact and wash your hands. Take photos of any damage for insurance.

What affects toilet repair pricing

Parts needed, access, whether the toilet must be pulled, and the time of day set the price. A flapper swap is a small job, and resetting a leaking base is larger. We give a clear price before work starts.
