Plumbing help in Monta Vista

Monta Vista is in the western part of Cupertino. The City of Cupertino General Plan divides it into Monta Vista North and Monta Vista South and describes a separate Monta Vista Village area. Our dispatch line takes calls from all of them.

Tell the dispatcher your street, the nearest cross street and whether you have a backup, a leak or a water heater problem. A plumber is sent the same day when one is available, and you get a clear price before work starts.

Where Monta Vista sits in Cupertino

The General Plan places Monta Vista North between Stevens Creek Boulevard to the north and Regnart Road and Regnart Creek to the south. Foothill Boulevard is the western edge and Highway 85 is the eastern edge. Lincoln Elementary, Kennedy Middle School and Monta Vista High School are in this area, along with Blackberry Farm and McClellan Ranch Preserve.

Monta Vista South runs from Regnart Creek toward Prospect Road and the Saratoga side. The plan describes hillside lots on the west, mostly over an acre, and valley floor lots of about 6,000 square feet on the east. Regnart Elementary, Three Oaks Park, Hoover Park and the 37-acre Seven Springs Ranch are here.

What the city says about the housing

The General Plan calls Monta Vista Village a farming and second-home community from the late 1800s. The City annexed it in steps from the 1960s and finished in 2004.

We do not guess what pipe sits behind any wall here. Pipe type depends on when a house was built, added onto or repiped, and only a look at your own plumbing answers that.

Hillside lots, long runs and what they mean for plumbing

This section is general plumbing information, not a measurement of any Monta Vista street. Water pressure at a fixture changes with elevation, so a house high on a slope can see lower pressure than one on the valley floor. A pressure regulator near the main shutoff sets what enters the house, and a gauge reading tells a plumber what is really happening.

On larger lots the buried line from the meter to the house is longer, which gives a leak more places to hide. A water bill that climbs with no change in use is a reason to check the meter and book leak detection.
