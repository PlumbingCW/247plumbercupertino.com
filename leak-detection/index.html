Water leaks rarely announce themselves. By the time a stain shows on a ceiling, the water has been running for a while, which is why our 24/7 line takes calls for emergency leak detection in Cupertino, CA. While you wait, shut off the main water valve if water is visibly flowing, switch off power to wet areas at the breaker, and move belongings out of the way.

Leak detection is the work of locating a leak accurately before anyone opens a wall or breaks a slab. It uses listening devices, thermal imaging, moisture meters and pressure tests so the repair is a small opening in the right place instead of a large opening in the wrong one.

When the water meter keeps moving

A water meter that moves when every fixture is off is the clearest sign of a leak somewhere past the meter. Shut off every tap, the dishwasher, the washing machine and the ice maker, then look at the meter's low-flow indicator. If it spins or the numbers climb, water is escaping.

A high bill with no change in habits tells a similar story. The leak may be a running toilet, a failing irrigation valve, a pinhole in a supply line under the house, or a break in the service line from the meter to the house. We isolate the system section by section until the leak sits in one stretch of pipe.

Ceiling, wall and attic leaks

A brown stain on a ceiling is water that has already traveled. The source may be a supply line, a drain joint above, a shower pan, or a roof leak that looks like plumbing. Thermal imaging shows cool wet areas behind drywall, and a moisture meter confirms them.

We trace the stain back to the source, not just the spot. A ceiling leak that is plumbing in origin is repaired by opening a small access point. A leak that traces to the roof is a different trade, and we tell you so.

Under-house and crawlspace leaks

Leaks under a raised foundation can run for months without any sign upstairs. Wet insulation, a musty smell, or a floor that feels spongy are the clues. We inspect the underfloor space with a light and a moisture meter, and a pressure test on the supply lines isolates the failing section.

A crawlspace leak left alone feeds mold and attracts pests, so a quick look is worth it when the signs are there.

What to do before we arrive: active leak

Turn off the main water valve, then open the lowest faucet to drain the lines. Switch off power to any wet area at the breaker. Photograph the damage for your insurance claim, and call the 24/7 line.

Buried line, service line and yard leaks

The line from the meter to the house is buried, and a leak there shows up as a soggy lawn, a sinking patch, or a meter that moves when the house is shut off. Acoustic listening on the line and a ground microphone narrow the spot, and a tracer gas or thermal pass can confirm it. We also check the meter box for leaks at the fittings, which is a simple repair.

Irrigation lines are a frequent hidden source. A broken sprinkler pipe or a stuck valve can run all night, and we test each zone. Pool and spa lines are a separate specialty, and we say whether a leak is in the plumbing or the shell.

What affects the price of leak detection

Access, the number of areas to test, the tools used and the time of day set the price. A single stain traced to a joint is quick. A whole house with several suspects takes longer. We give a clear price before work starts. Detection and the repair are quoted as separate items.
