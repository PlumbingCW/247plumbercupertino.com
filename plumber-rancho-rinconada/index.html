A neighborhood with older and rebuilt homes

Rancho Rinconada is in southeastern Cupertino. The General Plan lists it between Stevens Creek Boulevard, Tantau Avenue, Lawrence Expressway and Bollinger Road, with Fairgrove on its western side. Sterling Barnhart Park, Sedgwick Elementary and Cupertino High School are here.

None of that tells us what pipe is in your walls. A house can be original, added onto or fully rebuilt, and a plumber has to look at the actual system.

When a house has been added onto

This part is general plumbing information. When a home is remodeled, new lines are often tied into the existing ones, so one house can hold plumbing from more than one period. After a remodel, a slow drain or a drop in pressure can come from the point where old and new meet.

A plumber can trace what feeds what, and a camera shows the sewer line from the cleanout toward the street. If you are planning an addition, plumbing installation covers new fixtures, lines and permits.

Finding your sewer cleanout

A cleanout is a capped pipe that gives a plumber direct access to the sewer lateral. Depending on the house it may sit outside near the foundation, at the property line or in a garage or crawlspace. Knowing where it is saves time on arrival.

Do not open the cap during a backup, because sewage can come out under pressure. If you cannot find it, tell dispatch. The plumber can use another access point, such as a roof vent or a pulled toilet.

Sewer and drain calls

On smaller lots the sewer lateral usually runs a short distance between the house and the main in the street, though the route depends on the house. If more than one fixture backs up, the blockage is probably in the main drain or lateral rather than in one sink.

A sewer camera inspection shows whether the cause is roots, a sag or a break, so the fix is matched to the problem. For a plain clog, drain cleaning is usually the first step.
