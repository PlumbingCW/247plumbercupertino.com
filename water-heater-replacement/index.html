When a tank has split at the seam and water is spreading across the garage, the job is replacement and the clock matters. Our line for emergency water heater replacement in Cupertino, CA is open 24/7. Until we arrive, shut off the cold water supply valve above the heater, switch off the gas or electric supply, and move stored items out of the water.

Replacing a water heater means disconnecting and removing the failed unit, setting a new one, reconnecting water, gas or electric, venting, and fitting seismic straps. Done properly, it also includes a new relief valve discharge pipe and a final check for leaks.

A like-for-like tank is fastest. A move to tankless or heat pump changes the gas, vent and electrical work, and that can add a day. We quote each option so the comparison is not a guess.

Cupertino permit and code points

The Cupertino Building Division requires a plumbing permit for water heater replacement. Its instant online permit applies to standard tank gas heaters only, not tankless, with no HOA involvement and the same number of BTUs or fewer. Heat pump and tankless replacements go through the standard permit path, and the city says work without a required permit can be stopped and fees doubled.

California law also requires new and replacement water heaters to be braced, anchored or strapped against earthquake motion. We strap every unit we install, and the final inspection is easier when it is done right the first time.

Bay Area gas water heater rule

The Bay Area Air District adopted a zero-emission rule for residential gas water heaters that applies when a heater is replaced, and it does not require replacing a working unit. The 2023 rule set a January 2027 date, and the district has since adopted amendments moving the residential date to January 2028 and adding exemptions for qualifying low-income owners and for homes with space or electrical limits.

We recommend asking about a heat pump model before you buy a gas tank, and we check your panel and clearances before quoting either one.

Planning versus failing

If your heater is old and has not failed yet, replacing it on your schedule is easier than replacing it in a flood. Dates and rules change, so call to ask what applies before you choose a model.

No hot water at all

A heater that has stopped working entirely is not always a replacement. We diagnose first, and when it is a thermostat, an element or a gas valve, we repair it, which is covered under water heater repair. The tank itself decides: leaks and rusted bodies are replacement, failed parts are repair.

Cupertino homes and replacement logistics

Many homes in Cupertino have the heater in a garage or an exterior closet, and some townhomes have a closet with limited clearance. Access and venting decide whether a swap takes a morning or most of a day. Homes with HOA rules need approval, which the city says rules out the instant permit.
