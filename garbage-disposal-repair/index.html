A garbage disposal that hums without grinding, leaks onto the cabinet floor or backs up the sink can tie up the whole kitchen, so we answer the line for emergency garbage disposal repair in Cupertino, CA around the clock. Turn the switch off, flip the breaker off before you reach into the unit, and never put your hand in the disposal.

Disposal repair covers jams, motor and reset problems, leaks at the seals or the flange, clogs in the trap and drain past the unit, and replacement of a worn out disposal. Installation covers a new unit or a swap of an existing one, with the electrical and dishwasher connections set up.

Jammed or humming disposal

A hum with no grinding means the flywheel is stuck. A foreign object, a bone or a fruit pit often jams it. Many disposals have a hex socket on the bottom for turning the flywheel by hand and a reset button for the overload. With the power off, a plumber frees the jam and tests the motor.

A silent unit with no hum may have a tripped reset, a dead outlet or a failed motor. We test each, in order, so you do not pay for a new unit when a reset would do.

Leaks from a disposal

Leaks come from three places: the sink flange at the top, the dishwasher inlet, or the housing itself. A flange leak means the plumber's putty or the gasket has failed, and the fix is resealing. A housing leak means the unit has a crack or a worn seal, and replacement is the answer.

Water on the cabinet floor can look like a disposal leak and be a drain trap or supply line. We trace it before replacing anything.

Safety around a disposal

Turn off the power at the breaker before any hand work, even with the switch off. Never put a hand or a tool other than a long handled one into a disposal, and keep fingers away from the flywheel. If you smell burning or see smoke, shut off the breaker and call.

The clog behind the disposal

A disposal pushes ground waste into a drain that can clog. Fibrous scraps, coffee grounds and grease build up in the trap. A sink that backs up with the disposal on is usually clogged downstream, and the repair is cleaning the line, covered in drain cleaning.

Habits help: run cold water while grinding and for a short time after, and keep grease, bones, pasta and fibrous items out of the unit.

Choosing a disposal

Horsepower, grind stage and sound insulation are the main differences. A household that cooks a lot and runs it daily benefits from a larger motor and a good anti-jam design. A household that rarely uses a disposal can use a simpler model. We install the unit you choose or recommend one that fits your sink and budget.

What affects pricing

The unit, the repair or replacement, the electrical hookup and access set the price. A reset or a jam is a short visit, and a replacement is larger. We give a clear price before work starts.
